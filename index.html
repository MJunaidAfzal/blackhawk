About the Firm

A private family office with two major lines of business.

Blackhawk Partners, Inc. is a reliable trader and supplier of a wide range of commodities to industrial and financial consumers globally — and a firm that serves as both an advisor to, and an investor in, its clients.

Unlike many private family offices, venture groups and hedge funds, Blackhawk does not back ideas or start-ups. The Firm works aggressively with experienced operators seeking to partner for traditional buyouts, recapitalizations, growth and acquisition capital financing.

Our Mission

In a world awash with information, insight is often in short supply. Blackhawk’s edge is its ability to leverage the local know-how of its partners and professionals.

Blackhawk Partners’ mission is to leverage the insight and expertise of its team of advisory, investment and trading professionals to generate superior returns across a range of asset classes, while maintaining our good name and the good name of our investors.

While open to opportunities wherever they can be found, Blackhawk focuses on sectors in which it has access to intelligence second to none and a stellar group of operators — collaborating from deal sourcing and due diligence through company development and building long-term trading relationships.

Trading Guidelines

  • ContractsD2 · JP 54 · LNG · LPG · Crude
  • Spot dealsNot accepted
  • ProductImmediately liftable
  • Proof of FundsBetween principals only
  • PaymentRevolving Documentary L/C
  • CounterpartiesEnd users & title holders
  • DeliveryFOB only

We do not do business — directly or indirectly — with Iran, Syria, Nigeria, Ghana or Sudan. SBLC and BG payment mechanisms are not accepted.

Our Procedure

  1. 1
    Full Corporate Offer

    Seller issues an FCO on letterhead with a satisfactory refinery supply letter for buyer due diligence.

  2. 2
    Contract Issued

    After satisfactory due diligence, seller issues a contract for signing.

  3. 3
    Contract Signed

    Buyer signs and returns the contract following its conditions.

  4. 4
    Documentary L/C

    Buyer issues a one-month revolving DLC for the duration of the contract.

  5. 5
    Proof of Product

    Seller issues full POP; DLC pays against SGS, bill of lading and cargo manifest.

  6. 6
    Payment & Title Transfer

    Buyer’s bank pays seller’s bank, title transfers and the transaction is finalized.
